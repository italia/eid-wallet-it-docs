Attestation Onboarding
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

This section describes the processes that manage the claims, the schemas and the Credential types.
Each process is described with its Input, its Outcome and its Process.

Claim Registration
""""""""""""""""""

Claim Registration adds a standardized claim definition to the Claims Registry, when a claim that an Authentic Source or a Credential type needs is not yet available.
The Claims Registry is maintained by the Supervisory Body and it provides semantic definitions of the Credential attributes, such as their data type, their validation rules and their interoperability mappings.
The claim definitions are aligned to the :ref:`registry:Catalogue of Attributes`.
This process enables the :ref:`onboarding-system:Schema Provisioning` and the :ref:`onboarding-system:Credential Type Registration` that use the claim.

**Input**

The input is the definition of the claim to register, following the Claim Entry Parameters of the :ref:`registry:Claims Registry`, that is the canonical name of the claim, its description, its ``type`` (``string``, ``boolean``, ``array`` or ``object``), the ``format`` and the ``encoding`` where a string carries a qualified value, the ``aliases`` that map the alternative names used in other standards to the canonical claim, and the nested structure of an ``object`` or an ``array`` claim.
The canonical name and the aliases are aligned to the :ref:`registry:Catalogue of Attributes`.
The input is not the registration data of an Entity and it does not follow a profile of the :ref:`onboarding-system:Registration Data Model`, because a claim is a semantic definition and not an Entity.
The registry-level fields, that is the identifier of the registry, its version, its last-modification time and its localization configuration, are generated by the Claims and Schema Management.

**Outcome**

The claim definition in the Claims Registry, that the Authentic Sources, the schemas and the Credential types can reference.
A claim is never removed from the Claims Registry, because the Credentials already issued keep referencing it, so a claim that is deprecated is superseded by a new version, as described in :ref:`onboarding-system:Claims and Schemas Lifecycle`.

**Process**

1. An Authentic Source, during its :ref:`onboarding-system:Authentic Source Registration`, or a Credential Issuer, during a :ref:`onboarding-system:Credential Type Registration`, needs a claim that is not yet in the Claims Registry.
2. The definition of the claim is established, that is its canonical name, its ``type``, its ``format``, its ``aliases`` and its nested structure, aligned to the :ref:`registry:Catalogue of Attributes` and without conflicts with the claims already registered.
3. The Claims and Schema Management registers the definition in the Claims Registry.
4. The claim becomes available and it enables the :ref:`onboarding-system:Schema Provisioning` and the :ref:`onboarding-system:Credential Type Registration` that use it.

Schema Provisioning

Process that makes the schema of a Credential type available in the Schema Registry, either by generating it from the data model for nationally defined Credentials, or by registering it in alignment with an external Rulebook or standard.

Credential Type Registration
""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

.. note::
   Draft. To be written.

Process that creates a versioned entry of a Credential type in the Digital Credentials Catalog, with its data source and the reference to the applicable Rulebook.

Credential Type Activation and Deactivation
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

.. note::
   Draft. To be written.

Process that verifies the conditions for the issuance of a Credential type and moves its versioned entry between the active and the inactive state.

Credential Type Update
""""""""""""""""""""""

.. note::
   Draft. To be written.

Process that publishes a new version of a Credential type, activating the new versioned entry and deactivating the previous one.
