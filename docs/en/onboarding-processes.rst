Onboarding Processes
--------------------

This part gives the dynamic view of the onboarding.
Each process is described with its Input, its Outcome and its Process (the sequence of steps).

A process is described here only where the Onboarding System is the actor that carries it out and its outcome is a write on a registry or the issuance of a Trust Artifact.
Everything else, such as the subscription of an Authentic Source to PDND, the publication of an e-Service, the qualified status of a trust service provider, the certification of a Wallet Solution and the availability of the applicable Rulebook, is a precondition or a trigger and not a process.
The order of the processes in this part is thematic and not chronological, and the temporal order is given by the dependency map below.
